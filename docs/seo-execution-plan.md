# Worktree SEO execution plan

Status: Approved plan  
Last updated: 2026-08-21

## Purpose

This document turns `docs/seo-market-strategy.md` into an ordered delivery plan. The market strategy remains authoritative for target buyers, geographic posture, search territory, and positioning. This plan governs what Worktree should do next and what must be true before the program expands.

## Approved program outcome

Build a measurable search-acquisition system that:

- Attracts established mid-market companies seeking AI implementation and ongoing operating support.
- Competes nationally for AI implementation services, AI automation services, and managed AI services, with `AI implementation partner` as category language and commercial emphasis on the United States.
- Preserves Canadian reach through the shared national site.
- Captures Lower Mainland demand through a legitimate local search and Maps presence.
- Converts relevant search demand into qualified deployment-review inquiries.

## Operating constraints

- Choose and validate search intent before designing pages.
- Assign one page to each primary intent and prevent internal keyword cannibalization.
- Keep product and platform surfaces as proof of the service rather than repositioning Worktree as self-serve SaaS.
- Do not manufacture customer evidence, metrics, integrations, security capabilities, geographic presence, regulatory support, or outcomes.
- Do not create thin industry, city, or regional doorway pages.
- Keep the initial technical and content footprint small enough to verify before expanding.

## Phase 1: baseline and discoverability

### Outcome

Search engines can crawl, index, and measure the intended production site.

### Work

- Reconcile the deployed site with the repository's route and metadata contract.
- Restore or verify production `robots.txt`, sitemap, canonical metadata, social metadata, structured data, redirects, indexable commercial routes, and expected 404 behavior.
- Resolve production route gaps, including any intended product or publication routes that currently return 404.
- Verify Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools ownership.
- Submit the production sitemap to both services.
- Configure attribution for deployment-review conversions.
- Record a baseline for indexed pages, branded and non-branded queries, backlinks, country-level visibility, and existing organic conversions.

### Acceptance criteria

- `docs/seo-release-verification.md` passes against the production deployment.
- The submitted sitemap is reachable and contains only canonical, intended indexable URLs.
- Search Console and Bing can access the site without unintended blocking.
- A dated baseline exists before commercial SEO changes are evaluated.

## Phase 2: query validation and prioritization

Status: Complete — research ratified 2026-08-20

### Outcome

The approved search territory is converted into a prioritized query set based on current buyer intent and ranking opportunity.

### Work

For each approved query cluster, research the United States, Canada, and relevant Vancouver search results:

- Search-result composition and dominant page formats.
- Commercial, informational, software, and local intent.
- Competitor type, positioning, evidence, and authority.
- Available demand and ranking-difficulty estimates.
- Evidence buyers expect before conversion.
- Related questions, modifiers, and adjacent terminology.
- Material differences between United States and Canadian intent.

Score each candidate on:

1. Mid-market business-model fit.
2. Buyer and contract value.
3. Commercial intent.
4. Ranking feasibility.
5. Evidence readiness.

### Deliverable

Create `docs/seo-query-priorities.md` with the researched query set, scoring method, evidence, recommended priority, and deferred terms.

### Acceptance criteria

- Every priority query has a documented buyer intent and target market.
- Terms that attract self-serve, low-budget, or strategy-only demand are explicitly separated from the commercial core.
- The priority order is supported by current search-result evidence rather than terminology preference alone.

## Phase 3: keyword-to-URL architecture

Status: Complete. Approved architecture recorded in `docs/seo-query-url-map.md`.

### Outcome

Every important search intent has one responsible page and a defined relationship to adjacent pages.

### Work

- Inventory the current production and repository routes.
- Assign the homepage's primary query and commercial job.
- Decide whether existing product routes, new service routes, or consolidated routes should own each commercial intent.
- Assign the implementation, automation, managed AI, integration, comparison, security, deployment-review, and local intents using the ratified query clusters.
- Identify pages that should be retained, repositioned, consolidated, redirected, or excluded from indexing.
- Define the required internal-link relationships and navigation support.
- Prevent multiple pages from targeting the same query cluster without a distinct buyer job.

### Deliverable

Create `docs/seo-query-url-map.md` containing, at minimum:

- Target URL.
- Primary query cluster.
- Supporting queries.
- Search intent.
- Page job.
- Target buyer state.
- Required evidence.
- Primary CTA.
- Parent, sibling, and supporting links.
- Canonical and indexation status.

### Acceptance criteria

- Every primary and differentiated query from the market strategy has one page owner.
- No proposed page exists solely to repeat another page with different keywords.
- Redirects and canonical relationships are decided before copy or implementation begins.

## Phase 4: smallest complete commercial footprint

Status: Commercial copy and the Product-versus-Service implementation plan were revised after independent review. Implementation is awaiting approval of the revised plan, route-normalized copy, and operational claims.

### Outcome

Worktree has enough focused commercial coverage to test the approved search strategy without creating an oversized content program.

### Expected initial surface

The final architecture must come from Phase 3, but the first complete release is expected to cover:

- Homepage and category position.
- AI implementation services, supported by agent implementation and deployment variants.
- Managed AI services, differentiated through managed AI operations.
- AI automation services, qualified through AI workflow automation services.
- A comparison or buyer-decision page.
- Security and control evidence.
- Deployment-review conversion path.
- Vancouver/Lower Mainland local demand.

### Page brief requirement

Before drafting or implementation, each page must have a brief containing:

- Page job.
- Primary query and intent.
- Buyer context and desired operational change.
- Authoritative product and service facts.
- Available evidence.
- Required commercial and legal boundaries.
- Prohibited or unsupported claims.
- Primary CTA.
- Relationship to adjacent pages.

Use `docs/customer-centric-sales-copy-posture.md` as the governing copy system.

### Acceptance criteria

- Each page matches its mapped intent and does not compete with a sibling page.
- Metadata, heading structure, internal links, schema, and CTA support the page's actual job.
- Copy remains buyer-facing and evidence-led.
- The release passes the technical SEO contract before deployment.

## Phase 5: Lower Mainland local acquisition

### Outcome

Worktree can appear credibly in relevant Vancouver and Lower Mainland local search and Maps results without creating false geographic signals.

### Work

- Confirm Google Business Profile eligibility through genuine in-person customer contact.
- Select the accurate public-facing business identity.
- Verify the real Lower Mainland operating location.
- Configure the listing as a service-area business and hide the residential address when customers are not received there.
- Select the fewest accurate categories and realistic service areas.
- Connect the profile to the mapped local landing page with campaign attribution.
- Establish a compliant process for requesting reviews from real customers.
- Build consistent local citations and pursue relevant local partnerships and links.

### Acceptance criteria

- The profile complies with Google's eligibility, address, naming, and service-area rules.
- The linked local page contains substantive local relevance rather than inserted place names.
- Profile actions and local-page conversions are measurable.
- No fake United States location or unstaffed virtual office is used.

## Phase 6: evidence and authority

### Outcome

Worktree earns trust and links through real operating evidence rather than publishing volume alone.

### Priority evidence

- A recorded implementation or deployment walkthrough.
- A first case study with an agreed before-state and measurement method.
- Real operating artifacts such as workflow maps, approval policies, evaluation cases, run histories, and deployment records.
- Customer reviews and testimonials with permission.
- Clear implementation methodology and control boundaries.
- Relevant technical, business, and local partnerships.

### Expansion rule

New use-case, industry, regional, or educational pages should be added only when they have a distinct search intent and enough original evidence or expertise to deserve an independent result.

## Phase 7: measurement and expansion

### Monthly review

Measure:

- Crawl health and indexation.
- Rankings and impressions by United States, Canada, and Vancouver market.
- Non-branded clicks and click-through rates.
- Google Business Profile visibility and actions.
- Organic deployment-review starts and completions.
- Qualified versus unqualified inquiries.
- Pages gaining impressions without competitive click-through rates.
- Queries appearing without a suitable page owner.
- Links and citations acquired by page and market.

### Expansion criteria

Expand the footprint when at least one of the following is true:

- Search data reveals a material adjacent intent with no suitable page.
- A real deployment creates enough evidence for a new use-case or industry page.
- A shared national page underperforms in a material region because buyer needs differ.
- A successful page demonstrates a repeatable content and authority pattern.

Do not expand merely because competitors publish more URLs.

## Smallest complete test

The first strategy-validation release is complete when Worktree has:

1. A production deployment that passes the SEO release contract.
2. Search Console and Bing measurement with a recorded baseline.
3. An approved query-priority document.
4. An approved keyword-to-URL map.
5. Three focused national commercial page intents represented on the site.
6. One substantive Vancouver/Lower Mainland local page.
7. One compliant Google Business Profile connected to that page.
8. Conversion measurement for deployment-review inquiries.

The result should be evaluated as a system: crawlability, rankings, qualified demand, local visibility, and conversion quality.

## Dependencies

- Access to Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, and the appropriate analytics or conversion-measurement surface.
- A real Lower Mainland location and genuine in-person customer contact for Business Profile eligibility.
- Current search-demand or keyword-tool data where available.
- Product and platform proof produced by the parallel build effort.
- Customer permission and measurement evidence from the first design-partner deployment.

The technical foundation and query architecture can proceed before the first case study. Evidence-dependent expansion cannot.
