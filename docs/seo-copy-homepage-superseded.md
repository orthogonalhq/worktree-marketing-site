### Chapter 2: workflow gallery

**Eyebrow:** More capacity for real operational work

## Put AI to work where context has to become action.

The opportunity is rarely another place to type a prompt. It is recurring work that asks someone to gather the same context, prepare the same next step, and keep the same handoff moving. Worktree helps turn that routine into a managed workflow without removing the people who own the decisions.

#### Scene 01 — Executive follow-through

**Card headline:** Turn scattered requests into prepared next steps.

An illustrative workflow gathers the selected context behind a request, prepares the follow-through, and brings consequential decisions back to the owner.

**Expansion headline:** Your team asks. The routine handles what it can and makes the decision visible.

#### Scene 02 — Inbound inquiry preparation

**Card headline:** Give every serious inquiry a prepared human handoff.

An illustrative workflow reviews the submitted information, gathers the permitted business context, prepares the account brief, and places the next action with the right person.

**Expansion headline:** The inquiry arrives. The context and first follow-through are already moving.

#### Scene 03 — Customer account review

**Card headline:** Bring the signals behind an account into one review.

An illustrative workflow assembles selected account context, identifies missing information, and prepares a review for the owner before a decision or customer action is taken.

**Expansion headline:** The team sees the account, the open questions, and the next decision in one place.

#### Scene 04 — Finance exception preparation

**Card headline:** Prepare the evidence before the exception reaches a decision-maker.

An illustrative workflow gathers the selected records, highlights the discrepancy, and routes the review packet to the person with authority to decide.

**Expansion headline:** The routine gathers and compares. A person approves the consequential action.

#### Scene 05 — Recurring operating brief

**Card headline:** Assemble the inputs before the recurring review begins.

An illustrative workflow collects the agreed updates, identifies gaps, and prepares a brief the team can evaluate rather than rebuild from scratch.

**Expansion headline:** The review starts with the current context and the missing pieces already visible.
