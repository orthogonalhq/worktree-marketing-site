### Section: local working session

**Eyebrow:** Begin with the current process

## Put the real workflow on the table.

A useful first conversation is grounded in work that has already happened. Bring a recent example, the people who touched it, the systems they consulted, the decisions they made, and the result the business needed.

Together, the working session can clarify:

- What begins the workflow and who owns its result.
- Which context and systems the work depends on.
- Which steps are repeated and which require judgment.
- Where approval, ambiguity, and exceptions belong.
- What a useful result would look like.
- What must be true before implementation can begin.

The deployment review then turns that understanding into the next practical decision: clarify the process, assess technical feasibility, define an implementation, or decide that the workflow is not ready yet.

**Display direction:** Use an annotated workflow-intake board. Keep it operational rather than workshop-themed stock imagery.
