## Cross-page argument

The three service pages form one sequence without requiring the buyer to enter at the beginning:

1. **Automation:** Choose and improve the work.
2. **Implementation:** Put the workflow into controlled production.
3. **Managed AI:** Keep it useful and accountable after launch.

The homepage owns the Worktree relationship and routes visitors into that sequence. Vancouver owns local relevance and routes into the same national service pages rather than duplicating them.

The Vancouver copy can be reviewed now, but publication still requires the exact locality wording, practical in-person service area, hidden-address posture, public phone, hours, Business Profile URL, and local attribution implementation.

### Proof density

The first release will rely on designed artifacts and transparent implementation detail rather than customer outcomes. The homepage customer-story chapter and Vancouver local-proof section remain unpublished until permissioned evidence exists.

### Scope language

The service pages consistently begin with one bounded workflow. Review whether this feels focused and credible without making the long-term relationship appear artificially small.
